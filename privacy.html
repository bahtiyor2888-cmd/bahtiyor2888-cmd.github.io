Privacy Policy — YT Factory Studio

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

YT Factory Studio is used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not available to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, read the owner's channel and video statistics, and read and reply to comments on the owner's videos.

How data is stored

The access token is kept in the owner's private storage (the operating system's credential store and encrypted repository secrets). Statistics and comment texts are stored in the owner's private database only to show reports and draft replies. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. YouTube data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
